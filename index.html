About Maeghan

Stories told through movement.

Maeghan McHale creates contemporary work rooted in story, physicality, musicality and the full expression of the dancer.

Her choreography asks dancers to take up space, move with intention and step beyond familiar movement patterns. The goal is not simply to make work that looks beautiful, but to make work that reaches an audience and stays with them.
